Privacy Policy

SEEWELL respects user privacy and is committed to protecting the information associated with the use of the application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected during the use of the application.

Information Collection

The application may collect limited technical and usage-related information necessary for improving performance, validating algorithms, maintaining system reliability, and supporting research activities.

  • Anonymous analytics data
  • Visual acuity test responses
  • Test duration and error counts
  • Device model and screen resolution
  • Application performance metrics

Use of Data

SEEWELL may collect limited user and screening-related information required for clinical referrals, comprehensive screening support, research validation, and application improvement.

Personally identifiable information is securely stored using appropriate encryption and access-control measures. Such information is not publicly accessible and cannot be viewed, shared, or accessed by other application users without authorized permission from the SEEWELL administration or affiliated clinical team.

Research & Analytics

Information collected through the application may be used for:

  • Application improvement
  • Technical troubleshooting
  • Clinical validation
  • Statistical analysis
  • Research purposes

Any shared research data will be anonymized and handled in accordance with applicable institutional and ethical guidelines.

Data Protection

Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are implemented to help protect collected information from unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, or alteration.

However, no digital system can guarantee absolute security, and users acknowledge that use of the application is at their own risk.

Third-Party Sharing

Information collected through the application will not be sold or shared with unauthorized third parties. Data may be shared only with authorized research collaborators, institutional partners, or regulatory authorities when required for approved research, validation, compliance, or legal obligations.

User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that the application is used in accordance with applicable institutional policies, ethical standards, and local regulations.

If account-based access is enabled, users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials.

Policy Updates

IIT Madras Research Park reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the application following such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

About SEEWELL

SEE WELL is a digital visual acuity screening application developed by the Assistive Technology Team at IIT Madras Research Park in collaboration with Sankara Nethralaya.

The application enables accurate distance and near vision screening through smartphones and tablets using standardized logMAR-based and Snellen-equivalent charts.

Designed for use in hospitals, outreach eye camps, teleophthalmology programmes, and underserved rural communities, SEE WELL helps simplify and digitize the vision screening process.

Preliminary findings demonstrate strong agreement between app-based assessments and standard clinical visual acuity charts.

SEEWELL என்பது கண் பார்வைத் திறன் பரிசோதனைக்கான ஒரு டிஜிட்டல் பயன்பாடாகும். இது IIT Madras Research Park நிறுவனத்தின் உதவி தொழில்நுட்ப குழுவினரால் Sankara Nethralaya நிறுவனத்துடன் இணைந்து உருவாக்கப்பட்டது.

மருத்துவமனைகள், கண் பரிசோதனை முகாம்கள், தொலைக் கண் மருத்துவ சேவைகள் மற்றும் கிராமப்புற பகுதிகளில் பயன்படுத்த ஏற்ற வகையில் SEEWELL வடிவமைக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது.

ஆரம்பகட்ட ஆய்வு முடிவுகள், இந்த செயலியின் பரிசோதனை முடிவுகள் வழக்கமான மருத்துவ பார்வை அட்டவணைகளுடன் நெருக்கமான ஒற்றுமையைக் காட்டுகின்றன.
