SEEWELL Visual Acuity Testing Application

SEEWELL is a smartphone-based visual acuity screening application developed by the Assistive Technology Team, IIT Madras Research Park, Chennai, India. The application is intended exclusively for visual acuity screening and preliminary assessment purposes in clinical practice, research, training, educational settings, and community-based screening programs.

Medical Disclaimer

This application is not a substitute for a comprehensive eye examination performed by a qualified ophthalmologist or optometrist.

SEEWELL does not diagnose eye diseases, provide medical advice, prescribe treatment, or replace professional clinical judgment. The application is designed only to measure visual acuity using scientifically validated testing methods and calibrated display specifications.
