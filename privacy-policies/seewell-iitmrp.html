Privacy Policy

SEEWELL Visual Acuity Testing Application

SEEWELL respects user privacy and is committed to protecting information associated with the use of the application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected during use of the application.

Information Collection

The application may collect limited technical and usage-related information necessary for improving performance, validating algorithms, maintaining system reliability, and supporting research activities. Such information may include anonymous analytics data, visual acuity test responses, test duration, error counts, device model, screen resolution, and application performance metrics.

Personal Information

SEEWELL may collect limited user and screening-related information required for clinical referrals, comprehensive screening support, research validation, and application improvement. Personally identifiable information is securely stored using appropriate encryption and access-control measures. Such information is not publicly accessible and cannot be viewed or shared without authorized permission.

Use of Information

Information collected through the application may be used for:

  • Application improvement
  • Technical troubleshooting
  • Clinical validation
  • Statistical analysis
  • Research purposes

Any shared research data will be anonymized and handled in accordance with applicable institutional and ethical guidelines.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are implemented to help protect collected information from unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, or alteration. However, no digital system can guarantee absolute security, and users acknowledge that use of the application is at their own risk.

Information Sharing

Information collected through the application will not be sold or shared with unauthorized third parties. Data may be shared only with authorized research collaborators, institutional partners, or regulatory authorities when required for approved research, validation, compliance, or legal obligations.

Data Retention

Personal information collected through the SEEWELL application will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

All personal data will be automatically and permanently deleted from the application database after a maximum retention period of 90 days from the date of collection, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law, ethical review requirements, or authorized research protocols.

User Rights

Users have the right to request deletion of their personal data at any time before the expiration of the 90-day retention period. Upon receipt of a valid deletion request, SEEWELL will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize the requested personal information in accordance with applicable laws, institutional policies, research obligations, and regulatory requirements.

User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for ensuring that the application is used in accordance with applicable institutional policies, ethical standards, and local regulations. If account-based access is enabled, users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials.

Policy Updates

IIT Madras Research Park reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the application following such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
